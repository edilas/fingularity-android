Fingularity Privacy Policy

Android edition — updated October 18, 2026

Fingularity does not operate an account system, advertising network, analytics service, or developer-run game server.

Game data on your device

Level progress, local high scores (including any name you type for the on-device leaderboard), sound settings, tutorial state, and other gameplay preferences are stored locally by the app.

Google Play purchases

The optional “Endless Collapse” unlock is sold through Google Play. Google handles payment and purchase processing. Agos LLC does not receive your payment-card information. The app receives purchase and entitlement status needed to unlock the purchased content.

Google Play Games Services

If Play Games Services is enabled and available for your account, Fingularity can submit scores to its Google Play leaderboard and display the Play Games leaderboard interface. Google handles the associated Play Games profile and service data under Google’s privacy terms.

No ads or developer analytics

Fingularity contains no advertising SDK and no developer analytics or tracking SDK.

Children

Fingularity does not ask users to create an Agos LLC account and does not knowingly collect personal information on an Agos LLC server.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the published privacy-policy address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions: use the Agos LLC contact form at agosllc.com.
